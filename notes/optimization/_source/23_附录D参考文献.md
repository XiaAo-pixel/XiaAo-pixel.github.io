本讲义的内容以北京大学文再文老师 2026 年秋季最优化方法课程的课件为主线，课件对应的教材是 [1]。凸分析与凸优化模型主要参考 [2]，无约束优化的线搜索，信赖域与拟牛顿方法主要参考 [3]，复合优化与一阶算法的理论主要参考 [4, 5]。后面列出的论文是各讲中重要算法与例子的原始出处，供进一步阅读。

## D.1 教材与专著

[1] 刘浩洋, 户将, 李勇锋, 文再文. 最优化: 建模, 算法与理论. 北京: 高等教育出版社, 2020.

[2] S. Boyd, L. Vandenberghe. Convex Optimization. Cambridge University Press, 2004.

[3] J. Nocedal, S. J. Wright. Numerical Optimization. 2nd ed. Springer, 2006.

[4] A. Beck. First-Order Methods in Optimization. SIAM, 2017.

[5] Yu. Nesterov. Lectures on Convex Optimization. 2nd ed. Springer, 2018.

## D.2 论文

[6] M. J. D. Powell. On search directions for minimization algorithms. Mathematical Programming, 4: 193–201, 1973.（第 17 讲，分块坐标下降法的不收敛例子）

[7] T. Steihaug. The conjugate gradient method and trust regions in large scale optimization. SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis, 20(3): 626–637, 1983.（第 10 讲，截断共轭梯度法）

[8] M. J. D. Powell. How bad are the BFGS and DFP methods when the objective function is quadratic? Mathematical Programming, 34: 34–47, 1986.（第 11 讲，BFGS 与 DFP 的比较）

[9] O. Güler. On the convergence of the proximal point algorithm for convex minimization. SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization, 29(2): 403–419, 1991.（第 16 讲，近似点算法）

[10] J. Eckstein, D. P. Bertsekas. On the Douglas–Rachford splitting method and the proximal point algorithm for maximal monotone operators. Mathematical Programming, 55: 293–318, 1992.（第 18 讲，Douglas–Rachford 分裂）

[11] L. Qi, J. Sun. A nonsmooth version of Newton’s method. Mathematical Programming, 58: 353–367, 1993.（第 19 讲，半光滑牛顿法）

[12] Yu. Nesterov. Smooth minimization of non-smooth functions. Mathematical Programming, 103(1): 127–152, 2005.（第 14 讲，光滑化）

[13] A. Beck, M. Teboulle. A fast iterative shrinkage-thresholding algorithm for linear inverse problems. SIAM Journal on Imaging Sciences, 2(1): 183–202, 2009.（第 14 讲，FISTA）

[14] A. Chambolle, T. Pock. A first-order primal-dual algorithm for convex problems with applications to imaging. Journal of Mathematical Imaging and Vision, 40(1): 120–145, 2011.（第 16 讲，Chambolle–Pock 算法）

[15] S. Boyd, N. Parikh, E. Chu, B. Peleato, J. Eckstein. Distributed optimization and statistical learning via the alternating direction method of multipliers. Foundations and Trends in Machine Learning, 3(1): 1–122, 2011.（第 18 讲，ADMM）

[16] J. Bolte, S. Sabach, M. Teboulle. Proximal alternating linearized minimization for nonconvex and nonsmooth problems. Mathematical Programming, 146: 459–494, 2014.（第 17 讲，PALM 与 KL 性质）

[17] C. Chen, B. He, Y. Ye, X. Yuan. The direct extension of ADMM for multi-block convex minimization problems is not necessarily convergent. Mathematical Programming, 155: 57–79, 2016.（第 18 讲，多块 ADMM 的发散反例）

[18] X. Li, D. Sun, K.-C. Toh. A highly efficient semismooth Newton augmented Lagrangian method for solving Lasso problems. SIAM Journal on Optimization, 28(1): 433–458, 2018.（第 19 讲，半光滑牛顿增广 Lagrange 方法）
